⚙️ The mechanic

How it works

Four steps, and your missions organize themselves.

  1. 1

    Post a mission

    Title, category, starting price, decay per step, decay interval, and a criticality level: you decide.

  2. 2

    The price drops on its own

    Step by step, as long as nobody takes it, it loses value. The urgency badge climbs.

  3. 3

    Whoever takes it first wins

    At the price shown at that exact moment. A swipe confirms it once it's done.

  4. 4

    Spend your points

    In the reward shop you built together: movie night, sleep-in, pick-the-restaurant…

🔥 The rhythm

Spend it, or bank it. Never both

Your XP never drops: every completed mission earns you some, whether it's worth 3 or 30 points. Your levels climb through increasingly steep tiers. Your streak 🔥 holds as long as you complete at least one mission a day, a reminder pings you in the evening if it's at risk.

🪙 Unspent points → ⭐ Converted to XP (1:1)

Every Monday at 00:00 (local time), the spendable balance resets to zero. Nothing is lost: unspent points become XP. But they lose all purchasing power, hoarding never paid anyone's bills. A countdown warns you starting Sunday at 8pm.

❓ FAQ

What people ask us the most

Is it free?

Yes. Clinchy Basic supports up to 2 spaces, 6 members per space and 10 active missions (+1 per ad watched). Clinchy + unlocks the rest.

Does everyone need to pay?

No. Just one subscriber is enough: if the space's admin has Clinchy +, the whole space benefits (up to 50 members, unlimited missions). Free members keep their ads and hidden statistics.

How are points calculated?

Each mission has a starting price that loses value in steps as long as nobody takes it, based on an interval and a decay amount the creator chooses. Whoever takes it first earns the price shown at that moment.

What happens if nobody takes a mission?

Its value keeps dropping until it turns negative: it then becomes a penalty, a strong signal for someone to handle it quickly.

Why are my points reset every Monday?

Every Monday at 00:00 (local time), the spendable balance resets to zero and unspent points convert to XP at a 1:1 rate. Nothing is lost, but you have to spend to benefit: hoarding gives you no purchasing power.

Does it work for an 8-person shared house?

On Basic, a space supports up to 6 members. With a Clinchy + admin, the space scales to 50 members, plenty for a large shared house.

Can my kids use it?

Yes, through Kid Mode 🧸: a simplified interface with stars, inside a parent's account who stays in control. Exiting Kid Mode requires a long-press and a parent's approval.

Does it work offline / across multiple households?

A single account can belong to several spaces (household, shared house, team) and switch between them. The app needs a connection to sync activity between members in real time.

How do I invite someone?

With an invite code (e.g. HOME42) or a shareable link. The join request then needs to be approved by an existing member of the space, in real time.

How do I delete my account?

From the app (Profile → Settings → Delete my account) or, if you no longer have the app, through the public form on the account deletion page.
