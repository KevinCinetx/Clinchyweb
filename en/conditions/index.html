7. Points, missions and the rewards shop

The heart of Clinchy is a points economy internal to each space. It matters to understand what it is — and what it is not.

Clinchy points are a purely symbolic unit of account, internal to your space. They:

  • cannot be bought, and can under no circumstances be acquired with real money;
  • have no monetary value, and are neither a currency, nor electronic money, nor a digital asset, nor a claim of any kind;

  • are non-refundable, including when an account is closed, a space is deleted or the service is discontinued.

The Clinchy + subscription grants no additional points: it unlocks usage limits and features, never in-app currency.

7.2 How points are awarded

Every mission is posted with a starting value that decreases step by step for as long as nobody takes it. The member who accepts the mission acquires the value displayed at the moment of acceptance. If the value turns negative, the mission becomes a penalty for the member who was responsible for it. The scale, the steps and the decay speed are configured by the space administrators: they, not CINETX, decide the balance of their internal economy.

The spendable balance resets to zero every Monday at 00:00; unspent points are converted into XP, which feeds level progression and cannot be spent.

7.3 Rewards shop

A space’s shop is entirely created and stocked by its own members. The rewards (“a night off the dishes”, “pick the film”, “breakfast in bed”…), their wording, their price in points and their availability are defined freely by the space administrators.

Accordingly, CINETX is neither the seller, nor the supplier, nor the guarantor of the rewards. We provide the tool that lets members describe them and track their exchange; we take no part in defining or fulfilling them. Exchanging a reward for points is a private arrangement between members of the same space, with no contractual value towards CINETX.

A reward obtained but not honoured by the other household members is a matter for their private relationship alone. CINETX cannot be called upon to settle such a dispute, performs no arbitration and does not refund spent points.
