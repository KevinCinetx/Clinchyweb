💡 One subscriber is enough

That's the rule that changes everything: the subscription belongs to the space, not to each member. If the person who administers the space is Premium, the whole space gets 50 members and unlimited missions — other members don't have to pay anything to benefit.

How this plays out in practice

The admin subscribes → the entire space is unlocked (limits, ads). Free members of that space still keep their own ads and hidden personal statistics: Premium status unlocks the space's limits, not each member's individual experience.

What happens to a space if the admin unsubscribes?

Once the subscription expires, the space reverts to the free limits: 6 members and 10 active missions max. If the space had more members or missions at the time of the downgrade, access to Premium features is simply suspended — nothing is deleted, and a new subscription (from the admin or another member promoted to admin) immediately restores the expanded limits.

Restoring purchases

  1. Open Clinchy and go to Profile → Settings → Subscription.
  2. Tap "Restore purchases".
  3. Sign in if needed with the App Store or Google Play account used for the original purchase.

How to cancel your subscription

Clinchy doesn't handle billing directly — it goes entirely through Apple or Google. To cancel, at least 24h before the renewal date:

🍎

On iPhone / iPad

Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Clinchy → Cancel Subscription.

🤖

On Android

Google Play Store → Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Clinchy → Cancel subscription.

No prorated refund is issued by Clinchy: refund policy is handled exclusively by Apple or Google, depending on the purchase platform. Full details in our Terms of Use.
