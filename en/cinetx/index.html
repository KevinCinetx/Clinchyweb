Our app: Clinchy

Clinchy is a mobile app for managing household chores, built for couples, flatshares and families. It answers a very concrete problem: in a shared home, everyone knows what needs doing, but nobody wants to be the first to do it.

The principle: a reverse auction on chores

Clinchy applies the reverse auction mechanism (also known as a Dutch auction) to housework: every mission is posted with a starting value in points, and that value drops step by step for as long as nobody takes the mission.

The longer a chore lingers, the more urgent it becomes — and the stronger the incentive to grab it quickly. The first household member to accept the mission banks the value displayed at that exact moment. If nobody takes it, the value eventually turns negative: the mission becomes a penalty, which creates natural collective pressure without anyone having to play “chore manager”.

What it changes

  • No more mental load to divide up. The marketplace arbitrates instead of the people living there.
  • Measurable fairness. Points earned make each person’s real contribution visible, instead of a vague feeling.
  • A concrete reward. Points are spent in a rewards shop that household members define themselves.
  • A kid mode. A simplified interface, with stars instead of points, inside the account of a parent who administers the space.

Design

Clinchy uses a premium dark claymorphism: soft, tactile surfaces on a deep background (#13111E), raised cards (#221E36 and #2A2542), violet and blue accents, and an urgency colour scale — green, yellow, orange then red — that visually conveys a mission losing value over time.
