Delete your Clinchy account

Method 1: from the app (fastest)

1

Open your Profile

From the main screen, tap your avatar at the top of the app.

2

Go to Settings

Then scroll down to the "Account" section.

3

Tap "Delete my account"

You may be asked to sign in again if your session is old, for security reasons.

4

Type "DELETE" and confirm

Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and its identifier
  • Your profile and photo
  • Your points, XP, streak and level
  • Your mission and shop purchase history
  • Your membership in every space
  • Your preferences (theme, language, notifications)

What's kept, and why

Some data survives your account's deletion, for specific reasons:

  • Shared space content: the collective history (completed missions, activity) stays visible to other members, but your name is replaced with "Someone": we anonymize rather than punch holes in the rest of the group's history.
  • Billing records: kept for the duration of the applicable legal and accounting obligation.
  • Anonymized technical logs: kept for security and abuse-prevention purposes.

Maximum retention period for this residual data: 12 months.

⚠️ Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription

Deleting your Clinchy account does not cancel an ongoing Clinchy + subscription: Apple and Google keep billing it until it's cancelled separately.

On iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Clinchy → Cancel Subscription.
On Android: Google Play Store → Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Clinchy → Cancel.

Are you the sole admin of a space?

Before deleting your account, think about your space: if you're its only administrator, appoint a successor (promote another member to admin from the space's settings) or delete the space yourself. Otherwise, other members could lose access to the shared history.
