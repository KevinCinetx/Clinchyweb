🔒 Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026. Written in plain English, because you have the right to understand what we do with your data.

1. Who's responsible for your data

The data controller is {{PUBLISHER_NAME}}, located at {{PUBLISHER_ADDRESS}}, reachable at {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

2. Data we collect

  • Account: email address, first name, profile photo (optional)
  • Content you create: missions, rewards, activity history, statistics
  • Space membership: households, shared houses or teams you join
  • Technical identifiers: device ID, app version, operational logs
  • Advertising identifier: for free-tier users, subject to your consent

3. Why, and on what legal basis (GDPR)

  • Running the service (basis: contract performance): powering the mission marketplace, the shop, the statistics.
  • Security and abuse prevention (basis: legitimate interest): detecting abnormal behavior, protecting accounts.
  • Personalized advertising (basis: consent): Basic users who accept it only.
  • Product usage measurement (basis: legitimate interest): understanding which features are useful, in aggregate.

4. Who your data is shared with

We rely on subprocessors to run Clinchy:

  • Google Firebase: authentication, database, file storage
  • Google AdMob: ad delivery for the Basic tier
  • RevenueCat: Clinchy + subscription management
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: subscription payment processing

These providers may host data outside the European Union; in that case, the transfer is governed by Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.

5. Permissions requested on your phone

  • Notifications: for reminders and value-drop alerts.
  • Calendar & Reminders (iOS): to add a mission to your calendar; everything stays on your device.
  • Photos: only to choose your profile picture.
  • Ad tracking (App Tracking Transparency on iOS): declining doesn't prevent you from using the app; only ads become non-personalized.

6. Advertising

The Basic tier shows banner and rewarded ads. Clinchy + subscribers see no ads at all. In Europe, a consent screen lets you choose, and change your mind anytime from the app's settings.

7. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept as long as your account is active. You can request its deletion at any time from the Delete my account page.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, port, object to and restrict the processing of your data, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise them, write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. You may also file a complaint with your local data protection authority (for France, the CNIL).

9. Children

Kid Mode 🧸 is a simplified display inside an adult's account: it does not create a separate account. Clinchy isn't intended for autonomous use by a minor under {{MIN_AGE}} years old; it's the parent who creates and administers the space.

10. Security, changes, contact

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. This policy may evolve; the date at the top reflects the latest update. For any question: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

11. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, uses no trackers and integrates no analytics tool, which is why it doesn't show you a consent banner. Only the host's standard technical logs (IP address, timestamp) may apply, outside our direct control.
