2. Data we collect

Clinchy runs on Google Firebase. Here is exactly what is stored, and where:

  • Account (Firebase Authentication): your email address and a unique technical identifier (UID). Your password is never stored in clear text nor accessible to us: Firebase Authentication keeps only an encrypted hash of it. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive nothing beyond the email and identifier the provider passes on.
  • Profile (Cloud Firestore): your first name or nickname, your avatar, your theme, your notification preferences, your level and your XP.
  • Profile photo (Cloud Storage for Firebase): only if you choose one. It is optional — a generated avatar is offered by default.
  • Content you create (Cloud Firestore): missions, point values, shop rewards, activity history and statistics.
  • Space membership (Cloud Firestore): the households, shared houses or teams you join, and your role in each.
  • Technical identifiers: device ID, push notification token, app version and operational logs.
  • Advertising identifier: for free-tier users, and only subject to your consent.

We collect no location data, no contacts, no messages and no banking data: subscription payments are handled exclusively by Apple and Google, who pass us no payment details whatsoever.

3. Why, and on what legal basis (GDPR)

  • Running the service (basis: contract performance): powering the mission marketplace, the shop, the statistics.
  • Security and abuse prevention (basis: legitimate interest): detecting abnormal behavior, protecting accounts.
  • Personalized advertising (basis: consent): Basic users who accept it only.
  • Product usage measurement (basis: legitimate interest): understanding which features are useful, in aggregate.

4. Who your data is shared with

We rely on subprocessors to run Clinchy:

  • Google Firebase: authentication, database, file storage
  • Google AdMob: ad delivery for the Basic tier
  • RevenueCat: Clinchy + subscription management
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: subscription payment processing

These providers may host data outside the European Union; in that case, the transfer is governed by Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission. They act as processors: they handle the data on our behalf, on our instructions, and are not allowed to reuse it for their own purposes.

We do not sell your data. Ever.

CINETX does not sell, rent, transfer or trade any personal data with third parties, at any time, in any form, at any price. We do no data brokering either, and we share no data with data brokers or ad networks beyond the strict ad delivery described in § 6.

Our business model rests solely on the Clinchy + subscription and on advertising in the free tier. The only situation in which we could be compelled to disclose data is a judicial order from a competent French authority — and we would tell you about it, unless legally barred from doing so.

5. Permissions requested on your phone

  • Notifications: for reminders and value-drop alerts.
  • Calendar & Reminders (iOS): to add a mission to your calendar; everything stays on your device.
  • Photos: only to choose your profile picture.
  • Ad tracking (App Tracking Transparency on iOS): declining doesn't prevent you from using the app; only ads become non-personalized.

6. Advertising

The Basic tier shows banner and rewarded ads. Clinchy + subscribers see no ads at all. In Europe, a consent screen lets you choose, and change your mind anytime from the app's settings.

7. Retention and deleting your account

Your data is kept as long as your account is active. You can delete it at any time, without giving a reason, and without going through support.

From the app, in three taps

This is the fastest route, and it takes effect immediately:

  1. Open the Profile tab.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my account, then confirm.

From this website, if you no longer have the app

A public form, with no sign-in required, is available on the Delete my account page. Requests are processed within 30 days, with a confirmation email once the purge is complete.

What actually gets erased

Deletion is permanent and irreversible. It triggers:

  • deletion of your identity in Firebase Authentication (email and UID) — you can no longer sign in;
  • the purge of your profile document and all related documents in Cloud Firestore: missions, history, statistics, XP, space memberships, shop entries;
  • deletion of your profile photo in Cloud Storage for Firebase;
  • revocation of your push notification tokens.

If you are the last administrator of a shared space, the space and its content are deleted along with your account. If other members remain, the space continues to exist: the missions you had already completed stay there under a “deleted member” label, with no data that could identify you, so as not to distort the other members’ history.

Firebase’s encrypted backups may retain residual fragments for a short technical period before rotation, without them being reachable by the app. Data we are legally required to keep (billing, accounting obligations) is retained for at most 12 months, isolated and unusable for any other purpose.

Your Clinchy + subscription, if active, is managed by Apple or Google: deleting your account does not cancel it automatically. Remember to cancel it from your App Store or Google Play settings.

8. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, erase, port, object to and restrict the processing of your data, and to withdraw your consent at any time. To exercise them, write to admin.cinetx@getclinchy.com. You may also file a complaint with your local data protection authority (for France, the CNIL).

9. Children

Kid Mode 🧸 is a simplified display inside an adult's account: it does not create a separate account. Clinchy isn't intended for autonomous use by a minor under 13 years old; it's the parent who creates and administers the space.

10. Security, changes, contact

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. This policy may evolve; the date at the top reflects the latest update. For any question: admin.cinetx@getclinchy.com.

11. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, uses no trackers and integrates no analytics tool, which is why it doesn't show you a consent banner. Only the host's standard technical logs (IP address, timestamp) may apply, outside our direct control.
